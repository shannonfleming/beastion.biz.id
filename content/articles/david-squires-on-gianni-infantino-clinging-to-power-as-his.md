The world of football is no stranger to controversy, and the current situation at FIFA is a prime example. Gianni Infantino has been at the helm of FIFA since 2016, and his tenure has been marked by several high-profile controversies. One of the most recent and notable issues is the botched World Cup plan, which has led to widespread criticism and calls for his resignation. As the situation continues to unfold, it is clear that Infantino is clinging to power, but his grip is tenuous at best.
