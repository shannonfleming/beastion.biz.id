Ferran Torres, the Spanish forward, rose to fame after scoring the winning goal for Spain at the World Cup. Barcelona, his previous club, had been struggling to keep him, given the financial constraints and the need to balance their squad.

The transfer of Ferran Torres to PSG is a significant development in the world of football.
