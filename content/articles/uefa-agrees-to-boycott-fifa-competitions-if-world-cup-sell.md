## Introduction
Uefa has agreed to boycott all Fifa competitions if Gianni Infantino's plans to sell parts of the World Cup to private investors proceed. This decision was made during an emergency meeting where all 55 Uefa members unanimously voted against the proposal.
## Background
The proposal, which has been met with significant criticism, involves selling chunks of the World Cup to US-based private investment firms. This move has raised concerns among European countries, who believe it could compromise the integrity and values of the sport.
## The Boycott
The boycott would begin with the Under-20 Women’s World Cup and would include all future World Cups and Fifa-run events. Uefa members have stated that they "unanimously and unequivocally reject" the proposals, citing concerns over the potential consequences of private investment in the sport.
## Implications
This boycott could have significant implications for the world of football, potentially leading to a rift between European countries and Fifa. The move could also affect the sport's global popularity and revenue streams.
## Conclusion
The decision by Uefa to boycott Fifa competitions is a significant development in the world of football. As the situation continues to unfold, it remains to be seen how Fifa will respond to the boycott and whether the proposals will be revised or scrapped altogether.
