# Introduction
Manchester United, one of the most successful and beloved football clubs in the world, is facing a summer of financial restraint. With the current transfer window in full swing, the club's manager Michael Carrick has stated that they will not be making any £100m signings. This news comes as a surprise to many, given the club's history of splashing out on big-name players.

## Financial Constraints
According to Carrick, the club is unable to financially compete with their rivals, which has limited their ability to make major signings. This is a significant departure from the club's usual approach to the transfer market, where they have often been willing to spend big to attract top talent. The fact that they have already invested £85m in players like Youri Tielemans and Andrey Santos suggests that they are still committed to strengthening their squad, but they are being forced to be more prudent in their spending.

### Rashford's Return
Meanwhile, Marcus Rashford has rejoined the Manchester United squad after a loan spell at Barcelona. Despite his return, there is still uncertainty surrounding his future at the club. Carrick has insisted that Rashford is 'our player', but it is clear that both the player and the club are open to the possibility of him leaving. This situation is likely to be a major talking point throughout the summer, as Rashford is a key player for the club and his departure would be a significant blow.

## Implications for the Season Ahead
The fact that Manchester United are being forced to be more restrained in their spending has significant implications for the season ahead. The club will need to be clever and strategic in their transfer dealings, looking for value for money and identifying players who can make a real impact on the pitch. This could be a challenging task, given the intense competition for top players and the high prices being demanded by selling clubs.

### Conclusion
In conclusion, Manchester United's financial constraints are likely to have a major impact on their summer transfer dealings. While the club is still committed to strengthening their squad, they will need to be more prudent in their spending and look for value for money. The situation surrounding Marcus Rashford's future is also likely to be a major talking point throughout the summer, and the club will need to navigate this situation carefully to ensure that they get the best possible outcome. As the transfer window continues to unfold, it will be fascinating to see how Manchester United adapt to their new financial reality and how they approach the challenges ahead.
