Arsenal's transfer window has been a mixed bag so far, with the club experiencing both the thrill of a major signing and the disappointment of missing out on a key target. The recent news that Vinicius Jr will not be joining the Gunners has left fans wondering who might be the next big name to arrive at the Emirates.
