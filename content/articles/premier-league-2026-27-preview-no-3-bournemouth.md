# Premier League 2026-27 Preview: Bournemouth
The new Premier League season is just around the corner, and for Bournemouth, it promises to be an exciting and challenging campaign. After finishing sixth last season, the Cherries have a lot to live up to, and with a new head coach at the helm, the pressure is on to deliver.

## A New Era Under Marco Rose
Marco Rose, the former Borussia Mönchengladbach and Borussia Dortmund coach, has taken over the reins at the Vitality Stadium, and he has a tough act to follow. Last season's sixth-placed finish was a remarkable achievement, and the fans will be expecting similar success this time around. Rose has a reputation for playing attractive, attacking football, and he will be looking to impose his style on the team.

## European Debut
One of the most exciting aspects of the upcoming season for Bournemouth is their debut in European competition. After qualifying for the Europa League through their sixth-placed finish, the Cherries will be looking to make a splash on the continental stage. It won't be easy, but the experience will be invaluable for the players and the coaching staff.

## Squad Analysis
Bournemouth's squad is a mix of experienced players and young talent. The team has a solid core, with players like Dominic Solanke and Jefferson Lerma providing leadership and quality. However, the squad is not without its weaknesses, and Rose will need to address these areas if the team is to achieve success.

## Predicted Position
Our writers have predicted a 12th-placed finish for Bournemouth, which may seem pessimistic given last season's achievements. However, the Premier League is a highly competitive league, and there are many teams that will be pushing for a top-six finish. Bournemouth will need to be at their best if they are to achieve their goals.

## Conclusion
The 2026-27 season promises to be an exciting one for Bournemouth. With a new head coach, a European debut, and a talented squad, the Cherries have all the ingredients for success. However, the Premier League is a tough league, and nothing can be taken for granted. Bournemouth will need to work hard and be consistent if they are to achieve their goals and make it a season to remember.
