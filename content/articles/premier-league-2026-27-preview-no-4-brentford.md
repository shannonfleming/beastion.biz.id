# Premier League 2026-27 Preview: Can Brentford Build on Last Season's Success?
The 2026-27 Premier League season is just around the corner, and all eyes are on Brentford as they look to build on their impressive performance from last season. Under the guidance of Keith Andrews and with the goal-scoring prowess of Igor Thiago, the Bees outperformed expectations and finished 9th in the table.

## Last Season's Performance
Brentford's 9th place finish in the 2025-26 season was a remarkable achievement, considering the club's relatively small budget and lack of experience in the top flight. The team's high-scoring offense, led by Igor Thiago, was a key factor in their success. However, the challenge for Andrews and his team will be to replicate this form and avoid the dreaded 'second season syndrome'.

## The Challenge Ahead
The Premier League is notorious for its competitiveness, and Brentford will face stiff competition from established clubs with deeper pockets and more experienced squads. The likes of Manchester City, Liverpool, and Chelsea will be vying for the top spots, while other mid-table teams like West Ham and Crystal Palace will be looking to improve on their own performances.

## Key Players to Watch
Igor Thiago will once again be the main man for Brentford, with his goal-scoring ability and creative flair on the pitch. However, the team's success will also depend on the performances of other key players, including their defense, midfield, and goalkeeper. Keith Andrews will need to get his tactics right and make the most of his squad's strengths if they are to achieve a top-half finish.

## Predicted Position
The Guardian's writers have predicted an 8th place finish for Brentford, which would be a remarkable achievement considering the team's resources and experience. However, this will depend on various factors, including the team's form, injuries, and the performances of their rivals.

## Conclusion
Brentford's 2026-27 season promises to be an exciting one, with the team looking to build on their impressive performance from last season. With Keith Andrews at the helm and Igor Thiago leading the charge on the pitch, the Bees will be looking to outperform expectations once again and secure a top-half finish in the Premier League. Can they avoid the second season syndrome and achieve their goals? Only time will tell.
