# Fifa Pushes Ahead with Consultation Plan Amidst Backlash
Fifa has responded to the threat of a boycott from European nations with a statement saying that 'nobody is selling football', while blaming the media for disrupting its consultation process over controversial plans to sell chunks of the World Cup to US-based private investment.

## Background
After an emergency meeting on Thursday, Uefa member associations agreed to boycott all Fifa tournaments, including World Cups, unless the proposal was abandoned entirely. This move comes as a reaction to Fifa's plans to sell parts of the World Cup, which has sparked widespread criticism and concern among the football community.

### Fifa's Statement
Fifa claims that the 'process was disrupted by incorrect media reports', and insists that 'nobody is selling football'. The organization is pushing ahead with its consultation plan, despite the backlash from Uefa and other stakeholders.

## Uefa's Position
Uefa has taken a strong stance against Fifa's proposal, with its member associations agreeing to boycott all Fifa tournaments if the plan is not abandoned. This move highlights the deep divisions within the football community and the strong opposition to Fifa's plans.

### Implications of the Boycott
The potential boycott by Uefa member associations could have significant implications for the World Cup and other Fifa tournaments. If the boycott goes ahead, it could lead to a major crisis for Fifa and the football community as a whole.

## Conclusion
The controversy surrounding Fifa's plans to sell parts of the World Cup continues to escalate, with Uefa's threat of a boycott adding to the pressure on the organization. As the situation continues to unfold, one thing is clear: the future of the World Cup and the football community hangs in the balance. Fifa must now navigate this complex and challenging situation, and find a way to address the concerns of its stakeholders while also moving forward with its plans.
