# Introduction
Rangers' Europa League qualifying defeat by Jagellonia Bialystok has raised concerns about the team's performance under new manager Derek McInnes. This article will delve into the challenges facing McInnes and his side, exploring the factors contributing to their poor start to the season.

## Europa League Qualifying Defeat
The loss to Jagellonia Bialystok was a significant setback for Rangers, highlighting the team's struggles in European competition. Despite being favorites to win, Rangers failed to capitalize on their chances, ultimately succumbing to a disappointing defeat. This result has sparked questions about McInnes' tactics and the team's overall preparation for the match.

### Tactical Issues
One of the primary concerns for Rangers is their tactical approach under McInnes. The team has struggled to find a consistent rhythm, with players often looking unsure of their roles and responsibilities. This lack of clarity has led to confusion on the pitch, allowing opponents to exploit Rangers' weaknesses. McInnes must revisit his tactics and ensure that his players have a clear understanding of their duties and expectations.

## Player Performance
The performance of individual players has also been a concern for Rangers. Several key players have failed to deliver, with some struggling to find their form. McInnes must work with his players to identify areas for improvement and develop strategies to help them regain their confidence and composure on the pitch.

### Team Dynamics
Team dynamics have also played a role in Rangers' struggles. The team has lacked cohesion and unity, with players often appearing disconnected from one another. McInnes must focus on building a strong team culture, fostering a sense of camaraderie and shared purpose among his players. This will enable them to work together more effectively, supporting each other through the challenges of the season.

## Conclusion
Rangers' poor start to the season under Derek McInnes is a cause for concern, but it is not insurmountable. By addressing the tactical issues, improving player performance, and fostering a strong team culture, McInnes can help his team get back on track. With the right approach and mindset, Rangers can overcome their current challenges and achieve success in the Europa League and beyond.
