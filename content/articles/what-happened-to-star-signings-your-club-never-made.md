# Introduction
The world of football is often filled with excitement and anticipation, especially when it comes to transfer season. Fans eagerly await the arrival of new players, hoping that their team will sign the star they need to take them to the next level. However, not all transfer deals come to fruition. In this article, we will take a look at some star players who were close to moving to the Premier League, only for the deals to fall through.

## The Near Misses
Over the years, there have been numerous instances of high-profile transfers that failed to materialize. One such example is the proposed move of [Cristiano Ronaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo) to Manchester City in 2021. The deal was all but done, with the player having reportedly agreed personal terms with the club. However, the transfer ultimately fell through due to complications with the player's current club, Juventus.

Another notable example is the attempted signing of [Kylian Mbappé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylian_Mbapp%C3%A9) by Liverpool in 2017. The young French forward was heavily linked with a move to Anfield, but ultimately chose to join Paris Saint-Germain instead.

## Why Do Transfers Fall Through?
So, why do transfers that seem all but certain sometimes fail to materialize? There are a number of reasons, including:

* **Complications with the player's current club**: As seen in the case of Cristiano Ronaldo's proposed move to Manchester City, issues with the player's current club can often derail a transfer.
* **Disagreements over personal terms**: If a player and their potential new club cannot come to an agreement over wages, bonuses, and other personal terms, the transfer may not go ahead.
* **Intervention from other clubs**: If another club enters the fray and makes a late bid for the player, it can sometimes scupper the original deal.

## Conclusion
The world of football transfers is often unpredictable and full of twists and turns. While it can be disappointing for fans when a star signing fails to materialize, it is all part of the excitement and drama of the sport. As we look to the future, it will be interesting to see which star players will make the move to the Premier League, and which deals will fall through.
