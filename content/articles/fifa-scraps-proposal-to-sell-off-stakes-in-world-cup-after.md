# Introduction
The Fédération Internationale de Football Association (FIFA) has abandoned its proposal to sell stakes in the World Cup to private investors. This decision comes after a wave of criticism and opposition from various stakeholders in the football world.

## Background
FIFA President Gianni Infantino had put forth the proposal, which aimed to sell chunks of the World Cup to private investors. However, the plan was met with widespread anger and discontent globally, leading to a significant backlash against the idea.

### Opposition from UEFA and Other Confederations
The Union of European Football Associations (UEFA) had threatened to boycott future FIFA tournaments if the proposal was not shelved. Other confederations also came out in opposition to the plan, citing concerns about the potential impact on the sport.

#### Resignation of Senior Advisor
One of Infantino's senior advisors, Carlos Cordeiro, resigned on Friday in protest at the scheme. This move highlighted the deep divisions within FIFA regarding the proposal.

## Statement from Gianni Infantino
In a statement, Infantino confirmed that the proposal would not proceed, acknowledging that it had created divisions that were no longer in the interest of the objective. The FIFA chief operating officer, Kevin Lamour, had previously aimed a scathing rebuke at Infantino's lack of respect in seeking to force the proposal through with minimal consultation.

### Implications of the Decision
The abandonment of the proposal is a significant development in the world of football. It highlights the importance of consultation and cooperation among stakeholders in the sport. The decision also underscores the need for FIFA to prioritize the interests of the sport and its fans, rather than pursuing controversial and potentially divisive proposals.

## Conclusion
In conclusion, the scrapping of the proposal to sell stakes in the World Cup is a welcome development for football fans around the world. It demonstrates that FIFA is willing to listen to feedback and adapt its plans in response to criticism. As the sport continues to evolve, it is essential for FIFA to prioritize transparency, consultation, and cooperation to ensure that the interests of the sport and its fans are protected.
