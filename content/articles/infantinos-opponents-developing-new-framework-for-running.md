# Infantino's Opponents Unveil New Framework for World Football Governance
Uefa, Concacaf, and the Asian Football Confederation (AFC) are collaborating on a new framework for running world football, aiming to replace the current system of patronage that has been in place since Gianni Infantino took over as Fifa president 10 years ago.

## Background
Fifa has been under scrutiny for its governance, with repeated calls from Uefa, Concacaf, and the AFC for an independent review. However, these calls have been ignored by Infantino, prompting the opposing factions to take matters into their own hands.

### The Trigger
The recent plan to sell off 21% of the World Cup stakes, announced by Fifa a fortnight ago, has sparked widespread backlash. This move has been seen as a catalyst for the opposing factions to accelerate their efforts in developing a new framework for world football governance.

## The New Framework
The details of the new framework are still emerging, but it is understood that Uefa, Concacaf, and the AFC are working together to create a more transparent and accountable system. This new framework aims to address the concerns surrounding Fifa's governance and provide a more equitable distribution of power and resources.

### Key Objectives
The new framework is expected to focus on the following key objectives:
* Improving transparency and accountability within Fifa
* Enhancing the representation of all member associations
* Promoting a more equitable distribution of resources and power
* Strengthening the governance structure to prevent corruption and abuse of power

## Implications
The development of a new framework for world football governance has significant implications for the sport. If successful, it could lead to a more democratic and transparent system, where the interests of all member associations are represented. However, it also poses a challenge to Infantino's leadership and the current power structure within Fifa.

### Potential Outcomes
The outcome of this development is uncertain, but possible scenarios include:
* A gradual transition to a new governance system, with Fifa adopting elements of the new framework
* A more radical overhaul of the current system, with the new framework replacing the existing structure
* A prolonged period of conflict and power struggle between the opposing factions and Fifa

## Conclusion
The move by Uefa, Concacaf, and the AFC to develop a new framework for world football governance marks a significant turning point in the history of the sport. As the situation unfolds, it will be crucial to monitor the developments and assess the potential implications for the future of world football.
