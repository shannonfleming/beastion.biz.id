Eddie Howe's nearly five-year tenure as Newcastle United head coach has come to an end. The English manager, known for his tactical prowess and ability to develop young players, has left the Magpies, marking the beginning of a new era for the club.

Howe took over as Newcastle United head coach in November 2021, following the club's takeover by the Saudi Public Investment Fund. Under his guidance, Newcastle United also achieved a top-four finish in the Premier League, securing a Champions League spot for the first time in over two decades.
