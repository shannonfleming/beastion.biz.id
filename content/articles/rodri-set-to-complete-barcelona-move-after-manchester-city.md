Rodri, the 30-year-old Spanish international midfielder, is on the verge of completing a move to Barcelona after Manchester City accepted a bid of £65.4m from the Catalan club. Rodri has been an integral part of Manchester City's midfield since his arrival from Atletico Madrid in 2019. However, with his contract set to expire next summer, Rodri made it clear that he would like to explore opportunities back in Spain.

However, with Rodri's contract situation and his desire to leave, the club had little choice but to negotiate a deal. The transfer fee is a significant one, reflecting Rodri's importance to Manchester City and his value in the transfer market.
