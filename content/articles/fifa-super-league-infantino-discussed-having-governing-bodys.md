The term sheet reveals that Fifa was willing to sideline its member associations to push through plans to reduce the number of international windows.

### Key Points of the Term Sheet
- **Renaming the Competition**: The term sheet proposes that any new competition would be renamed under Fifa's branding, thus giving the governing body significant control over the project.
- **Sideling Member Associations**: Fifa was prepared to bypass its member associations to implement changes, including reducing international windows. This move would have given Fifa unprecedented power over the football calendar.
- **Golden Share**: The term sheet outlines that Fifa would have been granted a 'golden share', providing the governing body with special governance and political rights. This would have further solidified Fifa's influence over the competition.

## Implications of the Fifa Super League
The proposed Fifa Super League would have had far-reaching implications for European football. By rebranding the competition under Fifa's name, the governing body would have significantly increased its control over the sport.
