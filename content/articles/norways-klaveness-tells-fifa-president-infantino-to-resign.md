# Introduction
The world of football has been embroiled in controversy once again, with FIFA President Gianni Infantino facing renewed calls for his resignation. This time, the demand comes from the Norwegian Football Federation (NFF), which has become the first federation to directly request that Infantino step down.

## Background
The latest crisis to engulf FIFA stems from Infantino's attempt to sell stakes in the World Cup to private investors through his FIFA Forward Enterprise scheme. This move has been met with widespread criticism and has further eroded trust in the leadership of the organization.

### Norwegian Football Federation's Stance
At a meeting of the NFF board on Thursday night, the decision was made to ask Infantino to resign with immediate effect. This decision reflects Norway's long-standing commitment to scrutinizing FIFA's governance and its efforts to promote transparency and accountability within the organization.

#### Lise Klaveness's Statement
NFF president Lise Klaveness has been at the forefront of the criticism, stating that 'there is no way back for him.' This sentiment reflects the depth of feeling among many in the football community who believe that Infantino's actions have irreparably damaged his reputation and that of FIFA.

## Ongoing Controversies
Infantino has been battling for support around the world, but the latest developments have made his position increasingly untenable. The Norwegian FA's call for his resignation is likely to be followed by similar demands from other federations, further isolating the FIFA president.

### FIFA Forward Enterprise Scheme
The scheme, which aimed to sell stakes in the World Cup to private investors, has been widely criticized for its lack of transparency and potential for corruption. This has led to accusations that Infantino is more interested in pursuing personal and financial interests than in serving the best interests of football.

## Conclusion
As the pressure mounts on Infantino to resign, it remains to be seen how he will respond to the latest developments. One thing is certain, however: the Norwegian Football Federation's decision to call for his resignation marks a significant turning point in the ongoing saga surrounding FIFA's governance. With trust in the organization at an all-time low, it is imperative that meaningful reforms are implemented to restore faith in the beautiful game.
