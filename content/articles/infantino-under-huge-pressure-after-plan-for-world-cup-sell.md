# Infantino Under Fire: World Cup Sell-Off Plan Sparks Resignation and Rebuke
Gianni Infantino, the president of Fifa, is facing immense pressure after his plan to sell chunks of the World Cup to private investors has sparked a wave of opposition from around the globe. The latest developments have seen one of his senior advisers, Cordeiro, quit and brought a scathing rebuke from Fifa’s own chief operating officer, Lamour.

## Background
The plan to sell World Cup commercial rights to private investors has been met with widespread criticism. European countries have already promised to boycott Fifa competitions if the plan is not shelved. This has put Infantino in a difficult position, with his presidency hanging in the balance.

## Resignation and Rebuke
Cordeiro, a senior adviser to Infantino, has resigned in protest over the plan. This is a significant blow to Infantino, as Cordeiro was a key member of his inner circle. Additionally, Lamour, Fifa’s chief operating officer, has issued a scathing rebuke, stating that the plan ‘defies belief and imagination’.

## Opposition from European Countries
European countries have been vocal in their opposition to the plan. They have promised to boycott Fifa competitions if the plan is not shelved. This would have significant consequences for Fifa, as European countries are a major part of the organization.

## Pressure Mounts on Infantino
The pressure is mounting on Infantino to shelve the plan. With Cordeiro’s resignation and Lamour’s rebuke, it is clear that Infantino is losing support from within his own organization. The opposition from European countries has also made it clear that the plan is not viable.

## Conclusion
The World Cup sell-off plan has sparked a major crisis for Fifa and its president, Gianni Infantino. With opposition from European countries and criticism from within his own organization, Infantino is facing a difficult decision. Will he shelve the plan and save his presidency, or will he push ahead and risk losing the support of key stakeholders?
