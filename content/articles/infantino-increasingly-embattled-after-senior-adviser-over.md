# Infantino's Leadership Under Fire: Senior Adviser Resigns Over World Cup Sell-Off Plans

FIFA President Gianni Infantino's authority has been dealt a significant blow after Carlos Cordeiro, one of his senior advisers, resigned with immediate effect in protest at proposals to sell a stake in the World Cup.

## Background
The controversy surrounding the potential sell-off of the World Cup has been escalating, with 143 of FIFA's 211 members now standing against the scheme. This opposition has put Infantino's role as president under increasing pressure, with many questioning his leadership and decision-making.

## Cordeiro's Resignation
Cordeiro, the former US Soccer chief, criticized the proposed sell-off as 'a bad deal for football'. He pointed out that FIFA already sits on huge amounts of debt-free reserves, making the need for such a drastic measure unclear. Cordeiro's resignation is a significant embarrassment for Infantino, who has been facing opposition from European FAs threatening to boycott future FIFA tournaments.

## Implications
The resignation of Cordeiro and the growing opposition to the sell-off plans have significant implications for Infantino's leadership and the future of FIFA. The organization is already facing numerous challenges, including corruption allegations and criticism of its governance structure. The current controversy surrounding the World Cup sell-off has only added to the pressure on Infantino, with many calling for greater transparency and accountability within the organization.

## The Way Forward
As the situation continues to unfold, it remains to be seen how Infantino will respond to the growing opposition and the resignation of his senior adviser. One thing is certain, however: the FIFA president will need to address the concerns of the member associations and find a way to move forward that benefits the sport as a whole, rather than just a select few.

## Conclusion
The resignation of Carlos Cordeiro has dealt a fresh blow to Gianni Infantino's authority, highlighting the deepening crisis within FIFA. As the organization navigates this challenging period, it is essential that Infantino listens to the concerns of the member associations and works towards a solution that prioritizes the interests of the sport, rather than pursuing a controversial and potentially damaging sell-off of the World Cup.
