# Introduction to the Transfer News
The football world has witnessed significant transfers in recent days, with Chelsea and Brentford making headlines. ## Chelsea's Unconventional Signing of Danny Welbeck
Chelsea's decision to sign Welbeck marks a departure from their usual transfer strategy. The club has traditionally focused on acquiring younger talent with potential for development. However, the signing of Welbeck, a veteran striker, indicates a shift in their approach. This move is reminiscent of their 2022 transfer of Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang from Barcelona, where they paid a transfer fee for a player over the age of 26.

### What Welbeck Brings to Chelsea
Welbeck, with his wealth of experience, is expected to bring a new dimension to Chelsea's attack. His ability to play across various positions in the front line makes him a valuable asset to Xabi Alonso's squad. The 35-year-old striker has agreed to a two-year contract and will join the team on their pre-season tour.

### Sangaré's Potential Impact on Brentford
