# Introduction
Aston Villa's manager, Unai Emery, exuded confidence and positivity as he addressed the media on the eve of their Super Cup match against Paris Saint-Germain. Despite a summer of significant changes, including the departure of key players, Emery appeared relaxed and assured that his team would adapt and move forward.

## Emery's Jovial Mood
Emery's demeanor was notable, as he cracked jokes and poked fun at his captain, John McGinn. The Spanish manager's improved English was also on display, as he effortlessly navigated the questions from the press. However, beneath his jovial exterior, Emery was focused and alert, addressing the concerns surrounding the team's changes.

## A Season of Change
The summer has seen a significant overhaul of Aston Villa's squad, with several high-profile exits. However, Emery remains optimistic, citing the club's history of adapting to change and moving forward. He pointed to McGinn's example, who has been with the club since 2018 and has seen the team navigate the Championship and subsequent seasons of growth.

## McGinn's Intelligent Answer
When asked about the changes, McGinn provided a thoughtful response, highlighting the club's ability to evolve and improve despite the departures. Emery praised McGinn's intelligence and echoed his sentiments, emphasizing the team's capacity to absorb the losses and emerge stronger.

## The Super Cup Match
As Aston Villa prepare to face the European champions, PSG, in the Super Cup, Emery's confidence in his team is palpable. Despite the challenges ahead, he believes that his squad has the talent and resilience to compete with the best teams in the world.

## Conclusion
Unai Emery's positive outlook is a testament to his leadership and vision for Aston Villa's future. As the team embarks on a new season, they will undoubtedly face challenges, but with Emery at the helm, they are well-equipped to overcome them. The Super Cup match against PSG will be a significant test, but Emery's confidence and the team's determination make them a force to be reckoned with.
