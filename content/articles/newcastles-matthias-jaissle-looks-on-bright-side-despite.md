# Newcastle's Promising Start Under Matthias Jaissle Despite Leverkusen Defeat

Newcastle United's new head coach Matthias Jaissle had a lot to prove in his first major test at St James' Park, as his team took on German side Bayer Leverkusen in a friendly match. This meant that Jaissle had to rely on a youthful team that is still gelling together. The average age of the starting lineup was significantly lower than in previous seasons, and it showed at times, as the team struggled to find their rhythm and create scoring opportunities.

## Reasons to be Cheerful

Despite the defeat, there were plenty of reasons for Newcastle fans to be cheerful. The team showed glimpses of the exciting, attacking football that Jaissle has promised to bring to the club. The new head coach has been working tirelessly to implement his tactics and philosophy, and it's clear that the players are buying into his vision.

This suggests that Jaissle's team is capable of controlling games and creating opportunities, which is a positive sign for the future.
