# Infantino's Reign on Thin Ice: Uefa Members Prepare to Oppose Re-Election
The pressure on Gianni Infantino is mounting as Uefa members prepare to oppose his re-election as Fifa president. European nations are planning to withdraw their letters of support for Infantino, dealing a significant blow to his chances of retaining his position.

Infantino's reign as Fifa president has been marred by controversy, and the latest development is the collapse of his privately backed scheme to sell off chunks of the World Cup. This move was largely scuppered when Uefa member associations threatened to boycott future Fifa competitions if the plans proceeded.

## Uefa's Stance
The mood among European nations remains febrile, with a widespread appetite to ensure that Infantino is quickly removed from his post. The Uefa member associations have been vocal in their opposition to Infantino's plans, and their decision to withdraw letters of support is a clear indication of their intention to oppose his re-election.

## Implications
The implications of Uefa's stance are significant. Without the support of European nations, Infantino's chances of re-election are slim. The Fifa president has already faced significant opposition, and the latest development is likely to be the final nail in his coffin.

## Conclusion
In conclusion, Gianni Infantino's reign as Fifa president is on thin ice.
