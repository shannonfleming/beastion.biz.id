## Introduction
The European governing body, Uefa, has announced plans to commission an independent valuation of Fifa's World Cup sale plans. This move comes after concerns that Gianni Infantino, the president of Fifa, agreed to sell the tournament's future profits to a private equity firm at below the market rate.

## Background
Fifa's plan to sell the World Cup's commercial rights has been met with criticism from Uefa, who believe that the deal was made without proper consideration for the tournament's value. The European governing body has been vocal in its opposition to the plan, and has now taken steps to investigate the matter further.

## Uefa's Investigation
Uefa will commission an independent valuation of the World Cup's commercial rights to determine whether the deal made by Infantino was indeed below market value. This move is seen as a significant escalation of the dispute between Uefa and Fifa, and could have major implications for the future of the World Cup.

## Infantino's Apology
Infantino issued a written apology to Fifa's 211 member associations on Tuesday, conceding that the proposal was a 'mistake'. However, Uefa remains unmoved by the apology and will continue to push for his removal.

## Implications
The implications of Uefa's investigation are significant. If the independent valuation finds that the deal made by Infantino was indeed below market value, it could lead to a major scandal for Fifa and potentially even the removal of Infantino as president. The investigation could also have major implications for the future of the World Cup, with potential changes to the tournament's commercial rights and governance structure.

## Conclusion
Uefa's decision to investigate Fifa's World Cup sale plans is a significant development in the ongoing dispute between the two governing bodies. The outcome of the investigation could have major implications for the future of the World Cup, and could potentially even lead to the removal of Gianni Infantino as president of Fifa. As the situation continues to unfold, one thing is certain - the future of the World Cup is hanging in the balance.
