# Premier League 2026-27 Preview: Everton FC
The 2026-27 Premier League season is just around the corner, and Everton FC is looking to build on their 13th-place finish from last season. With a new air of stability surrounding the club, the Toffees have made some modest but sensible recruitments during the summer transfer window.

## Recruitment and Transfers
Everton's recruitment this summer has been characterized by a sense of pragmatism and caution. Rather than making any flashy, big-name signings, the club has focused on bringing in players who can provide depth and consistency to the squad. This approach may not have generated much excitement among fans, but it is a sensible one given the club's current financial situation and the need to avoid another relegation battle.

## Predicted Position
According to the Guardian writers' predictions, Everton is expected to finish 10th in the Premier League this season. This is a modest improvement on last season's 13th-place finish, but it is still a relatively conservative prediction. While the Toffees have the talent and experience to push for a top-seven finish, they will need to be consistent and avoid the kind of slump that plagued them in the spring of last season.

## Last Season's Performance
Everton's 13th-place finish last season was a disappointment, especially given the high expectations that surrounded the club at the start of the campaign. However, the Toffees did show some promising signs, particularly in the early part of the season. With a stronger squad and a bit more luck, they should be able to improve on last season's performance and push for a top-10 finish.

## Managerial Prospects
David Moyes is back at the helm of Everton FC, and he will be looking to prove himself as a top-tier manager once again. Moyes has a reputation for being a fighter and a motivator, and he will need to draw on all his experience and expertise to get the best out of his players this season. If the Toffees can avoid another slump and stay consistent, Moyes may find himself in the running for a European spot come the end of the season.

## Conclusion
In conclusion, the 2026-27 season promises to be an interesting one for Everton FC. With a new air of stability surrounding the club and some sensible recruitments during the summer, the Toffees are well-placed to improve on last season's performance and push for a top-10 finish. However, they will need to be consistent and avoid the kind of slump that plagued them in the spring of last season. With David Moyes at the helm and a bit of luck, Everton FC can achieve great things this season and make their fans proud once again.
