## Liverpool FC Ownership Stake Sold to Consortium Including Jeff Bezos

In a significant development, Liverpool FC's owner, Fenway Sports Group (FSG), has confirmed the sale of a 30% stake in the club to a consortium including Amazon founder Jeff Bezos, Facebook co-founder Eduardo Saverin, and Amit Bhatia. The deal, valued at £1.65bn, places the overall value of the club at £5.5bn.

### Consortium Led by Amit Bhatia

Amit Bhatia, the son-in-law of Indian steel magnate Lakshmi Mittal, led the talks with FSG on behalf of the 1892 Holdings consortium. Bhatia, a former co-owner of Queens Park Rangers, has received financial backing from the Mittal Family Trust, the K5 Sports fund (led by Jeff Bezos), and EE Capital (the family office of Elaine and Eduardo Saverin).

### New Vice-Chairman and Expanded Board

As part of the deal, Amit Bhatia will become the club's new vice-chairman on an expanded board. This move is expected to bring new perspectives and expertise to the club's decision-making process.

The sale of a 30% stake in Liverpool FC to the consortium is a significant development in the world of football. With the involvement of high-profile investors like Jeff Bezos and Eduardo Saverin, the club is likely to benefit from increased financial resources and expertise.
