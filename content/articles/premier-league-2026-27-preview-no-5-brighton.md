# Premier League 2026-27 Preview: Brighton

The Premier League 2026-27 season is just around the corner, and Brighton is gearing up for what promises to be an exciting yet challenging campaign. With the loss of key players, including Welbeck and Van Hecke, the team is in need of squad depth to cope with the demands of European football.

## Last Season's Performance

Brighton finished 8th in the Premier League last season, a respectable position that highlighted the team's potential. However, with the departure of crucial players, the team's dynamics are likely to change, and it remains to be seen how they will adapt to these changes.

## Predicted Position

According to Guardian writers, Brighton is predicted to finish 9th in the upcoming season. While this is not necessarily a bad position, it does indicate a slight decline from last season's performance. The prediction is based on the average of the writers' tips, providing a general consensus on the team's expected outcome.

## Challenges Ahead

One of the significant challenges Brighton faces is the loss of key players. Welbeck and Van Hecke were instrumental in the team's success last season, and their departure leaves a significant gap that needs to be filled. The team's management will need to work diligently to bring in new talent and strengthen the squad to cope with the demands of European football.

## The Need for Squad Depth

European football is notoriously demanding, with teams required to play multiple matches in a short span. This can be physically and mentally exhausting, making it essential for teams to have a deep and talented squad. Brighton will need to invest in quality players to ensure they can compete at the highest level and avoid burnout.

## Conclusion

The Premier League 2026-27 season promises to be an exciting one for Brighton, with the team facing both challenges and opportunities. While the loss of key players is a significant setback, it also provides a chance for new talent to emerge and for the team to grow and develop. With the right squad depth and strategy, Brighton can overcome the challenges ahead and achieve a successful season.
