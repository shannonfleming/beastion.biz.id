# Championship Roundup: Middlesbrough's Dramatic Comeback and Notable Results

The Championship has kicked off with a bang, featuring several exciting matches that set the tone for the season. One of the most notable results was Middlesbrough's comeback win against Lincoln, a team that had been unbeaten for nine months.

Middlesbrough fought back to secure a hard-fought 2-1 victory against Lincoln, ending the latter's remarkable unbeaten run. The Imps made a strong start, opening the scoring in the 12th minute through Adam Reach. However, Will Lankshear, a former Tottenham forward, marked his debut with a second-half double, equalizing just after the hour and then scoring a stoppage-time winner.
