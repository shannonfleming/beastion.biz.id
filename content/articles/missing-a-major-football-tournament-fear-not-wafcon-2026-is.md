# Introduction to Wafcon 2026
The Women's Africa Cup of Nations, also known as Wafcon, is just around the corner, and it's an event you won't want to miss. With the men's World Cup having come to a close, football fans can now shift their attention to the exciting tournament that's about to unfold in Morocco.

## The Tournament Schedule
The 34-match event will take place between 26 July and 16 August, perfectly positioned between the conclusion of the men's World Cup and the start of the league season in Europe. This timing ensures that football enthusiasts can enjoy continuous high-quality competition without a break.

## Participating Teams and Favorites
Several teams are expected to perform well in the tournament, including Cape Verde and Egypt, who are looking to capitalize on the feel-good factor generated by their men's teams in the World Cup. However, the team to beat is Nigeria, as they aim to secure their 11th title in the competition.

### Why Wafcon 2026 Matters
Wafcon 2026 is more than just a tournament; it's an opportunity for African women's football to shine on the global stage. The event provides a platform for talented players to showcase their skills, potentially attracting the attention of top clubs and leagues worldwide.

#### The Host Country: Morocco
Morocco, the host nation, is well-prepared to welcome teams and fans from across Africa. With its rich football history and state-of-the-art facilities, Morocco is an ideal location for Wafcon 2026.
