# Introduction
The world of football has been abuzz with controversy surrounding FIFA President Gianni Infantino's proposed plan to sell off stakes in the men's World Cup to private investors. The plan has been met with widespread criticism, and Infantino has come under pressure to step down. However, in a surprising turn of events, US President Donald Trump has come out in support of Infantino, warning that removing him would be a 'terrible mistake'.

## Background
Infantino's plan to sell off World Cup stakes has been widely panned, with many critics arguing that it would undermine the integrity of the sport and prioritize profits over the interests of fans and players. The plan has been seen as a major misstep for Infantino, who has been under fire for his handling of various issues during his tenure as FIFA President.

## Trump's Support
In a post on his social media platform, Truth Social, Trump expressed his support for Infantino, calling him a 'fantastic' leader who has done a great job for FIFA. ### Implications
Trump's support for Infantino is significant, given the US President's influence and profile. However, it is also possible that Trump's support could backfire, given the controversy surrounding his own presidency and the perception that he is interfering in the affairs of an international sporting organization.

## Conclusion
The controversy surrounding Infantino and FIFA is likely to continue, with many questions still unanswered about the future of the organization and the sport as a whole.
