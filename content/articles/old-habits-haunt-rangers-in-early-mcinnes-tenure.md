# Introduction
The Europa League qualifying first-leg defeat to Jagiellonia Bialystok has left Rangers and their new manager Derek McInnes with a lot to ponder. Despite the optimism surrounding McInnes' appointment, old habits seem to be haunting the team, with the loss of cheap goals being a major concern.

## McInnes' Assessment
McInnes was forthright in his assessment of the team's performance, stating that Rangers were 'guilty of beating ourselves'. This sentiment echoes the frustrations of the fans, who have grown accustomed to seeing their team concede avoidable goals. The manager's words suggest that he is aware of the team's shortcomings and is determined to address them.

### The Problem of Conceding Cheap Goals
The issue of conceding cheap goals is not a new one for Rangers. Under previous managers, the team has struggled to maintain a solid defensive shape, often allowing opponents to capitalize on individual errors. McInnes will need to work on instilling a greater sense of discipline and organization in his team if they are to overcome this problem.

## The Challenge Ahead
The defeat to Jagiellonia Bialystok has left Rangers with a mountain to climb in the second leg. The team will need to be more clinical in front of goal and tighter at the back if they are to overturn the deficit. McInnes will be looking to his players to respond positively to the setback and show the kind of resilience and determination that has been lacking in recent performances.

### The Importance of Team Selection
McInnes' team selection will be crucial in the second leg. The manager will need to choose a lineup that can balance defense and attack, while also being mindful of the need to score goals. The team's attacking players will need to be more clinical in front of goal, while the defense will need to be more solid and compact.

## Conclusion
The Europa League qualifying first-leg defeat to Jagiellonia Bialystok has highlighted the challenges facing Rangers under Derek McInnes. The team's old habits of conceding cheap goals and lacking discipline have resurfaced, and it will be up to McInnes to address these issues. With the second leg looming, Rangers will need to show a significant improvement if they are to progress in the competition. The fans will be hoping that McInnes can inspire his team to a turnaround in form and help them overcome the obstacles that have been holding them back.
