# Introduction
Charlton Athletic, a London-based football club, has been put up for sale by its US owners, led by Gabriel Brener. This move comes just three years after the ownership group acquired the club for £15m in 2023. The decision to sell the club has sparked interest among potential investors, with Oakwell Sports Advisory engaged to find a suitable buyer.

## Background
Charlton Athletic has experienced a significant amount of change in its ownership structure over the past six years, with the club having had four different owners during this period. The current ownership group, led by Brener, has decided to cash in on their investment, providing an opportunity for a new owner to take the reins and shape the future of the club.

## Sales Deck
A sales deck sent to potential investors, obtained by the Guardian, highlights Charlton Athletic as 'the last remaining football club upside opportunity in London'. The deck emphasizes the potential for significant equity growth through promotion to the Premier League, commercial expansion, and investment in women's football. According to Oakwell, new owners could see a return of five to 10 times their initial investment, making Charlton Athletic an attractive opportunity for investors.

## The Valley Stadium
The Valley Stadium, home to Charlton Athletic, provides a unique opportunity for a new owner to invest in a London-based football club with a rich history and dedicated fan base. The stadium, with a capacity of over 27,000, offers a solid foundation for the club to build upon and expand its commercial operations.

## Potential for Growth
The potential for growth at Charlton Athletic is significant, with the club currently competing in the English Football League (EFL). Promotion to the Premier League would provide a substantial increase in revenue, while investment in women's football and commercial expansion could further enhance the club's financial position.

## Conclusion
The sale of Charlton Athletic presents a unique opportunity for a new owner to invest in a London-based football club with significant potential for growth. With the right investment and leadership, the club could experience a resurgence in fortunes, both on and off the pitch. As the search for a new owner begins, fans of Charlton Athletic will be eagerly awaiting the next chapter in the club's history.
