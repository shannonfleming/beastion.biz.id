### Introduction
The English Football Association (FA) has formally withdrawn its support for Gianni Infantino's re-election as the president of the Fédération Internationale de Football Association (Fifa). This decision comes after the Football Association of Wales (FAW) dropped its backing for Infantino's candidature, citing a loss of confidence in his leadership.

### Background
Infantino has been facing mounting pressure and criticism during his tenure as Fifa president, with several European countries expressing their dissatisfaction with his leadership. The FAW's decision to withdraw its support marked a significant turning point, as it was one of the first nations to publicly denounce Infantino's candidacy.

### The FA's Decision
The English FA has now followed suit, confirming that it will no longer back Infantino for a fourth term in charge. This move is expected to be followed by other European countries, as they attempt to force Infantino's resignation. The Football Association of Finland was the first nation to withdraw its support last week, and more are likely to join the growing list of nations opposing Infantino's re-election.

### Implications
The withdrawal of support from the English FA and other European countries is a significant blow to Infantino's chances of re-election. As the pressure mounts, it remains to be seen whether Infantino will be able to withstand the criticism and retain his position as Fifa president. The fate of his presidency hangs in the balance, as the football community waits with bated breath for the outcome of this developing story.

### Conclusion
The English FA's decision to withdraw its support for Gianni Infantino marks a significant turning point in the ongoing saga surrounding the Fifa president's re-election. As more nations join the growing list of countries opposing Infantino's candidacy, it remains to be seen whether he will be able to survive the mounting pressure and retain his position as the head of world football's governing body.
